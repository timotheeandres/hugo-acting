Today, I see the school I was attending, Acting Line Studio, close its doors. Through these two years I spend there, I have met amazing people, discovered many things about acting, learnt a lot about myself, and made—I hope—many long-lasting friendships.

But contrary to what most people think, it is not a shame I did not finish this formation, and it is not with sadness that I say goodbye to this school. Contrary to more classical formations, this was never about getting a paper. It was about growing myself into a better actor than the day prior, and knowing myself. Those two years are not wasted, or lost. They're past. And knowing the people who were in charge of the school, this is definitely not the end of the road. A hardship, for certain. But not the end.

I do not know where life will take me from now on. But I will cherish those two years, those hundreds of hours of work, and I hope someday I'll be able to publicly announce in a microphone: "I was there".
